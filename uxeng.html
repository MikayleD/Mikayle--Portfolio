First, I tackled the task of reorganizing the existing playgrounds. In user interviews many engineers heavily apreciated our documentation and always went to the bottom portion called the 'playground' where one can play with the properties of a component.

Results

Before

After

As you can see in the screenshots all of the playgrounds have now been moved to the top of every story, increasing efficiency as now engineers are more easily able to play around with the props of specific components they need.



Results


Before

In this example there was not a prior playground that fully functioned, so the new version actually works and includes new props that werent updated in documentation.

Impact

The new playgrounds were a hit with the front-engineers, with feedback collected being that the addition of playgrounds being at the top make them more accessible. They have been able to test out components necessary for their work more efficiently and have been able to play around with the props of components that were not previously documented.

What I would do Different:

This project was assigned to me during the last 3 weeks of my internship but with more time allotted I would've love to expand it out more. I would have liked to have done more user testing with the new playgrounds to see if there were any other features that could be added to make the playgrounds even more efficient.
